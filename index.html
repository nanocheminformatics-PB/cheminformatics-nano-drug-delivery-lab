ABOUT THE LAB

Connecting disciplines.
Advancing discovery.

Our laboratory works at the interface of computational chemistry, bioinformatics, medicinal chemistry and nanotechnology. We aim to combine predictive molecular methods with experimental and translational research to investigate biomedical questions.

This website is the lab’s public research hub. Project descriptions, team profiles and publications should be updated with verified information by the lab administrator.
